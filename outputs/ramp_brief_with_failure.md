## Expensify

**Pricing:** The free tier provides individual expense tracking without any workspace features. It includes unlimited SmartScans with no monthly cap, distance tracking, manual expense entry, and export to CSV or spreadsheet formats. Receipts can be forwarded to receipts@expensify.com for automatic scanning. Expensify price plans have limits based on SmartScans, not the number of persons.

**Core features:**
- Individual expense tracking
- Unlimited SmartScans
- Distance tracking
- Manual expense entry
- CSV or spreadsheet export

**Positioning:** Expensify is positioned as expense management software for individuals, freelancers, and employees tracking company-related costs, and it also serves businesses handling expenses, travel, and corporate cards. The results emphasize ease of use and workflow simplification, with integrations and automated receipt scanning.
